      Privacy & method

      Your birth details travel to this server in a POST request, not a URL. The application does not intentionally retain personal requests or results. The shared cache contains weather only. No account, analytics, or third-party browser requests are used by this page.

      Optional device storage remembers settings, never calculated results. “Forget my details” clears this page’s settings and the active older profile; other older profiles are retained.

      Weather: Open-Meteo, with data attribution under CC BY 4.0. Astronomy: Swiss Ephemeris through pyswisseph and Kerykeion, with an explicit Moshier backend. Calculation and timezone versions appear with each result.

      Moon sextiles and trines to your natal Moon, Venus, or Jupiter add one personal contribution; squares and oppositions subtract one. Only exact events during the session count, capped at ±2. Weather bands come first, then personal timing, shared Moon timing, gusts, rainfall, and earlier start.

      Your fishing horoscope offers authored interpretations of computed contacts to your natal Moon, Mercury and Mars, read at each displayed session’s midpoint within 2°. They are not measured mental states, they do not predict catches, and they do not affect which time ranks first. “Recurring” only means the same contact appears at two or more displayed midpoints.

      Birthplace lookup sends the typed city name to Open-Meteo’s geocoder. No birth date or birth time is sent to the lookup provider.
