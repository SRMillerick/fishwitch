Affiliate disclosure

how baromoon makes money — and how it doesn't

The rule

Monetization never touches ranking. Every recommendation on this site is scored by a deterministic pipe — weather, astronomy, lake state, and a cited knowledge base. Retailer links are attached after the ranking is complete. No advertiser, brand, or retailer can buy position, and scores never see offers.

What the links are

  • Manufacturer pages — product citations from the knowledge base. No commission, ever.
  • Affiliate links (where present) — labeled as such, always. If you buy through one, baromoon may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.
  • The gap lane ("the gap in your tackle box") — items the engine scored highly for your conditions that aren't in your stated tackle box. Same scoring as everything else; the link is how you fix the gap, not how the item got there.

What we don't do

  • No paid placements, sponsored posts, or "featured" slots.
  • No selling or sharing of user data (we don't have any — see privacy).
  • No link shorteners that hide destinations.
