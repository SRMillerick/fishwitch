How it works

  1. Weather + history — forecast for the window, a 75-day archive behind it: heat streaks, water-temp estimate, and turnover inference (when a lake mixes, deep fish and slow presentations win).
  2. Sky math — Swiss-Ephemeris sunrise/sunset, moon, solunar majors/minors, planetary hours; optionally transits to your natal chart.
  3. Knowledge base — every lure category carries conditions (light, wind, water temp), provenance, and where it came from: manufacturer catalogs or state-agency guides. Read the citations →
  4. Scoring — fixed arithmetic over all of it. The output is a ranked set of windows, prime moments, and the two rods that fit. Deterministic: you can re-derive every number.
