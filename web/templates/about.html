About baromoon

named for the two signals that survive calibration

baromoon is a deterministic fishing-report engine. It reads the sky (Swiss-Ephemeris astronomy: sun, moon, solunar periods), the weather (forecast plus a 75-day archive that infers lake turnover), and a cited knowledge base of lure and rig conditions — then scores every candidate window and tells you when to be on the water, where, and what to tie on.

Why "baromoon"

Because those are the two signals our calibration ledger has actually validated: barometric phase (the post-frontal rise versus the descent into a trough) and the moon (dark-moon feeding windows, solunar majors). Everything the engine claims, it claims because logged trips — catches and skunks alike — earned it. When the data disagrees with the model, we fix the model.

The discipline

  • Deterministic piping. Same sky, same weather, same lake → the same report, every time. No AI writes these facts at runtime.
  • Knowledge is data. Every lure entry carries provenance — manufacturer catalogs and state-agency guides, cited link by link. Humans verify every promotion.
  • Monetization never touches ranking. Tackle links attach at the end of the pipe, always disclosed.
  • Your data stays yours. Profiles live in your browser. See privacy.

Voices

The same math, three vocabularies: fisher (plain English, zero astrology), almanac (moon-sign lore), astro (full natal transits — if you want the sky read against your own chart).
