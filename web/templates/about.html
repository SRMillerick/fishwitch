About baromoon

named for the two signals that survive calibration

baromoon is a fishing report built on three things: the sky (sun, moon, solunar periods), the weather (forecast plus a 75-day archive that tracks heat and turnover), and a tackle library of lure and rig conditions — every entry cited to a manufacturer catalog or a state-agency guide. It scores every candidate window and tells you when to be on the water, where, and what to tie on.

Why "baromoon"

Because those are the two signals our calibration ledger has actually validated: barometric phase (the post-frontal rise versus the descent into a trough) and the moon (dark-moon feeding windows, solunar majors). Everything baromoon claims is something logged trips — catches and skunks alike — have earned. When the data disagrees with the model, we fix the model.

The discipline

  • Same inputs, same report. No AI writes the facts at runtime — the numbers are computed from the sources, every time.
  • Every claim has a source. Lure and rig entries cite manufacturer catalogs and state-agency guides, link by link. A person reviews each one before it goes live.
  • Money never touches the ranking. Tackle links attach only after the picks are set, and they're always disclosed.
  • Your data stays yours. Profiles live in your browser. See privacy.

Voices

The same math, three vocabularies: fisher (plain English, zero astrology), almanac (moon-sign lore), astro (full natal transits — if you want the sky read against your own chart).
