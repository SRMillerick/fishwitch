Earlier pentacle studies (not chosen)

Your reference is a triskelion-pentacle decal; Etsy is bot-walled from this box, so these are the common constructions of that family. All are single-ink and token-driven. The honest caveat: pentagram + spiral + ring is a lot of linework for 16px — the small-size column will tell us which ones survive.

T1 · Triskelion pentacle (layered classic)

Ring + pentagram + triple spiral at the hub. The literal reading of the reference; the most esoteric, the most detail at small sizes.

T2 · Triskele in a ring

The triple spiral alone in a ring — the older half of the reference, no pentagram. This is the one I'd bet survives 16px.

T3 · Crescent triskelion

Three crescents in rotational symmetry — moon-forward, almanac, and it quietly keeps the triple rhythm without a pentagram.

T4 · Pentacle + moon

Ring + pentagram with a crescent set in the hub: the cleanest witchcraft read, and the moon carries the brand name.

T5 · Pentacle + phases

A node on each point — reads as full/empty moon phases at large sizes and as a studded star at small ones. The five nodes are where a future phase animation could live.

T2 at favicon size (small-size test)

What the triskele looks like when it actually has to be a browser tab. If the spiral arms merge into a pinwheel here, the mark needs a heavier cut for the icon.

Alternate directions

R0 · Triskele (current)

Three arcs. Reads as an almanac ornament, but there's no moon, no water, no barometer — and at 16px it becomes a knot.

R1 · Isobar moon

Moon with two pressure bands beneath — weather-map isobars that double as water. Both halves of the name, one glyph.

R2 · Needle dial

Barometer face with a needle and barb; the cardinal ticks read as a compass. Most "instrument," least "moon."

R3 · Rise over water

Crescent over water. The simplest read at 16px; reads "night fishing" before it reads anything else.

R4 · Gauge + moon

A ¾ gauge arc with the moon in the gap. Instrument-first; the moon is a detail rather than the subject.

R5 · Ring phase

A full ring with a phase inside. The most "moon," the least barometer, and about as simple as a mark gets.

R6 · Wave crescent

Crescent riding a wave. The fishing read is direct; the wave may fight the table-heavy surfaces in the app.
