How it works

Solunar tables stop at the moon. baromoon runs the whole lake — weather, history, water, cover, sky, and tackle — and shows the arithmetic.

  1. Weather + history — forecast for the window, a 75-day archive behind it: heat streaks, water-temp estimate, and turnover inference (when a lake mixes, deep fish and slow presentations win).
  2. Sky math — Swiss-Ephemeris sunrise/sunset, moon, solunar majors/minors, planetary hours; optionally transits to your natal chart.
  3. Tackle library — every lure and rig shows its conditions and its source: manufacturer catalogs or state-agency guides. Read the citations →
  4. Scoring — fixed arithmetic over all of it, ranked into the ledger. Re-run it and you get the same numbers.
