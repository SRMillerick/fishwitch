What is lake stratification, and what does turnover do to the fishing?

In summer many lakes split into layers and the water below the stratification level loses oxygen; fall cooling mixes them again and sends bass deep.

Many lakes stratify at 6 to 20 feet in summer. There is no oxygen or fish below the stratification level, so the fishable water is the oxygenated layer above it. As fall progresses and the water cools to the low-to-mid 50s, bass return to deep water as the lake mixes.

That mixing event — turnover — is why post-turnover fishing shifts deep. baromoon watches for it and flags the lake state in the report.
