The Abstract (6th Sense)

urchin/dice-style bait family (separate from the Neko rig); owner cross-refs: Hideout Coike, Berkley Omoeba, Hag's Prickly Pear

The bait

Styleurchin · barrel-shaped body with flat ends; wide flat appendages; urchin-style
Sizes12mm · 18mm · 24mm · 28mm
MaterialHyperStretch
Buoyancyenhanced
Weight slotintegrated nail-weight slot
Rigs wacky · trig · urchin

Rigging rule: the hook needs to be larger/wider than the central ball of that kind of bait; if the hook doesn't extend beyond that central ball, you will lose fish or miss your hookset

Field notes (owner observations)

  • owner rig: 24mm green/blue Abstract, 3/0 treble hook, two 1/8 oz tungsten screw nail weights (1/4 oz total)
  • owner: medium-heavy rod required — lighter rods feel loaded like weeds; casting is challenging (poor aerodynamics)
  • owner: bite is sudden, hookup instant/hard
  • owner buoyancy: neutral — TPE urchin baits weigh a bit but won't sink unless weighted (maker copy says 'floating')
  • Jack's setup (2026-09-13): 24mm green pumpkin blue Abstract, 1/0 treble, 3/16 oz VMC nail weight — 12 lb (lake-record class) plus 7/6/5/3/3 in one Sunday session
  • Jack's line (2026-09-13): braid tied directly to the hook with no leader — against the finesse-leader convention, and it still produced the 12 lb
