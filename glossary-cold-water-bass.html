How do you catch bass in cold water?

Below about 50°F bass feed little and sit deep; stay in contact with the bottom and move slowly.

As a lake cools through the low-to-mid 50s, bass return to deep water, and feeding is greatly reduced below 50°F. Winter fish are slow, so the productive approach is a soft plastic that moves slowly along the bottom, fished with contact. Some winter bass suspend and will take smaller prey presented right in front of them.
