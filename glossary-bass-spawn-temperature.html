When do bass spawn, and where do they stage?

Largemouth bass stage as the water nears about 55°F and spawn in the 60–75°F range; the male then guards the nest for 10–14 days.

Staging begins as water warms to about 55°F, and between 55 and 62°F bass move shallow to feed aggressively. Nesting happens in the 60–75°F band; the guarding male stays on the nest 10 to 14 days while the eggs incubate and hatch.

After the spawn, females sometimes slide out to deeper edges and secondary points. baromoon's spawn layer supersedes the month-based season fits while a phase is active.
