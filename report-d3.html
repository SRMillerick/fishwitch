Line & knots for these rigs

  • Monofilament (nylon) — buoyant and stretches under load, which damps the surge on a hooked fish Knots quoted from the cited guides — facts, not folklore.
  • San Diego jam knot (line-to-terminal) — The San Diego jam knot is a common fishing knot used to tie a line to the hook, swivel, clip, or artificial fly. This knot is also known as the San Diego knot, reverse clinch knot or Heiliger knot. (Wikipedia — San Diego Jam knot)
  • Palomar knot (line-to-terminal) — The Palomar knot is a knot that is used for securing a fishing line to a fishing lure, hook, or swivel. It is strong and easy to tie and with practice can even be tied in the dark. (Wikipedia — Palomar knot) Practice: always wet knots before tightening, regardless of line material; fluoro under 10 lb: 10 wraps when tying the snell or uni.
